=====================================================
NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES INC.
=====================================================
Financial Report — Fiscal Year 2024
=====================================================


EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
---------------------------------
Nexora Technologies delivered strong growth in fiscal 2024, driven by expansion 
into new markets and increased enterprise adoption of its cloud platform.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q3 2024 FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS
-----------------------------------
- Total Revenue: $5.2 million
- Operating Expenses: $3.1 million
- Gross Profit: $2.1 million
- Profit Margin: 40.4%
- Customer Acquisitions: 320 new enterprise clients
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): $1,200 per client
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q4 2024 FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS
------------------------------------
- Total Revenue: $6.8 million
- Operating Expenses: $4.0 million
- Gross Profit: $2.8 million
- Profit Margin: 41.2%
- Customer Acquisitions: 410 new enterprise clients
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): $1,056 per client (down 12% from Q3)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-------------------
ANNUAL SUMMARY 
-------------------
- Total Annual Revenue: $22.4 million
- Total Annual Expenses: $14.1 million
- Annual Profit Margin: 37.1%
- Total New Clients: 1,200 enterprise clients
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS
--------------------
- Expanded into 3 new markets: Saudi Arabia, Germany, and Brazil
- Launched NexoraCloud 2.0 with AI-powered analytics in Q4
- Reduced infrastructure costs by 18% through serverless migration
- Employee headcount grew from 180 to 240
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RISKS AND CHALLENGES
--------------------------
- Increased competition in the EU market
- Rising cloud infrastructure costs in Q3
- Regulatory compliance costs in new markets estimated at $400,000
